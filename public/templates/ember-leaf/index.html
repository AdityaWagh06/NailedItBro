Our story

A family recipe book, a charcoal fire and a lot of patience.

Ember & Leaf started as Sunday dinners for friends. Today it is a 60-seat dining room, but the rules haven't changed: grind the spices fresh, give the dal all night, and never rush the naan.

We buy from local farms when we can and change the menu four times a year.

Chef Meera Rao
